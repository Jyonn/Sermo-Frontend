# FRIENDEN Living Wallpaper System

## Product Intent

The LV17 wallpapers are small inhabited worlds. Architecture provides a calm, readable
chat surface while animals and plants behave according to their own habits. Motion must
communicate life, never camera movement or generic decoration.

## Scene Stack

Every theme is authored in the original 941 by 1672 coordinate system and rendered by one
responsive SVG with \`xMidYMid slice\`:

1. Environment plate: complete architecture with animated actors and major plants removed.
2. Back vegetation: rooted plants that can move behind characters and furniture.
3. Character layer: one transparent file and one independent behavior per animal.
4. Front vegetation: flowers and leaves that restore depth in front of characters.
5. Semantic effects: water droplets and sleep puffs generated by the scene runtime.
6. Chat UI: messages, composer, and chrome always remain above the wallpaper.

The original single-image wallpaper is not sampled or clipped at runtime.

## Character Behaviors

Night Rooms:

- Door rabbit rises from a compressed hiding pose, pauses, and looks around.
- Window cat alternates alert posture and small head-weight shifts.
- Sleeping dog and sheep breathe; sleep puffs appear only during settled phases.
- Bear and window cat periodically lean toward their mugs.
- Penguin dozes with a slow imbalance-and-recovery cycle.
- Bird uses a two-peck sequence with a clear return to rest.
- Bottom cat stretches horizontally before settling.

Afternoon Conservatory:

- Window characters peek independently instead of moving in sync.
- Watering dog shifts its weight, tilts the can, and releases timed water drops.
- Sunhat rabbit uses a short repeated nibble posture.
- Sleeping spotted dog breathes and produces sparse sleep puffs.
- Bouquet carrier balances the sunflower while changing stance.
- Letter carriers lift and settle their envelopes.
- Tea rabbit pours while its companion leans in to watch.
- Basket carrier uses an alternating weighted step cycle.

## Plant and Insect Behaviors

Vines rotate around their root attachment, arches expand asymmetrically, broad leaves fan,
flower heads nod, hanging plants lag behind their pot, and sunflowers use a slower tracking
arc. Butterflies combine path movement, body rotation, and horizontal squash to suggest
wing beats; they do not share the drifting-particle system used by atmospheric themes.

## Runtime Rules

- Scene definitions own asset URI, source coordinates, dimensions, layer, behavior, phase,
  and optional semantic effects.
- Behaviors have long rest windows and independent negative delays to avoid synchronized
  movement.
- \`IntersectionObserver\` and document visibility gate all animation.
- Reduced-motion mode keeps the layered composition but freezes every behavior.
- CSS environment plates remain valid fallbacks while transparent layers load.
- The same React component is used by live chat, conversation previews, and welcome-message
  previews.

## Asset Pipeline

\`scripts/build-frienden-living-assets.py\` converts generated source plates and atlases into
production assets. It:

- exports compressed environment plates;
- slices every actor and plant into an independent transparent image;
- removes low-alpha studio halos and re-expands edge alpha;
- trims transparent bounds with safe antialiasing padding;
- keeps deterministic asset names consumed by the scene manifest.

Generated source atlases remain outside the application bundle. Only processed environment,
actor, plant, and insect assets are committed.

## Authoring Constraints

- Never animate a rectangle cropped from a completed wallpaper.
- Never leave the same actor or animated plant visible in the environment plate.
- Never reuse generic translation for unrelated species or activities.
- A character action must show anticipation, active phase, recovery, and rest.
- Plants must preserve a believable root or pot anchor.
- New effects must belong to an action, such as water or sleep, not ambient visual noise.
- Test both themes at portrait mobile size and wide desktop crop before release.
